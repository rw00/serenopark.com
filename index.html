The Venue

The perfect setting for precious and important celebrations

Located alongside the Hasbani river, Sereno offers an enchanting outdoor venue for weddings, birthdays, and celebrations of every kind.

We collaborate with caterers, DJs, and lighting partners to craft a celebration tailored to your vision and style.

Whether it's an intimate gathering or a grand party, Sereno is where unforgettable memories are made.

The Pool

Sun, water & pure relaxation

Escape the ordinary and dive into tranquility. Our pool area is a serene oasis surrounded by lush gardens and the gentle sounds of the Hasbani river.

Soak up the sun and relax in the jacuzzi.

The Park & Café

Nature, flavors & good vibes

Savor the best of spring and summer along the river. Fire up the grill, breathe in the cool air, and spend quality time with the people who matter most.

Enjoy tasty sandwiches and cold drinks from our café. Planning a picnic? Bring your own food and rent our BBQ kit for the ultimate picnic experience!

Frequently Asked Questions

Sereno means serenity in Spanish – and that's exactly the experience we deliver.

At Sereno, it's all about good vibes, great company, and unforgettable moments.

We have our own snack café where we sell sandwiches and beverages.

For park picnics, you're welcome to bring your own food and use our BBQ grills.

For the pool, please only order from our café.

We can comfortably host up to 350 guests.

A large area remains available as a dance floor, along with a stage for event performances or shows.

For information on venue rental pricing and event packages, please contact us directly.

We help plan your entire event! From catering and drinks to music, lighting, tables, and chairs — we partner with trusted local providers to bring your celebration to life.

For casual park days, our café offers a selection of hot and cold beverages, fresh sandwiches, and BBQ grill kits available for rent.

The Park is open every day from 11:00 AM to 11:30 PM during Spring, Summer, and on clear-weather days.
